SAFARI EXTENSION

Focus Tube

YouTube without Shorts and endless scrolling.

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Focus Tube.
  2. Enable the extension and allow access to YouTube.
  3. Open m.youtube.com in Safari. The green Focus Tube screen or “filter on” confirms it is working.

If you use the earlier Userscripts filter, keep only one copy active. Use the account link on YouTube to switch accounts.
